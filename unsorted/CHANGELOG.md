NABLOOLIFE - UPDATE LOG: TOKYO DRIFT

Midnight. A parking lot. A car that wants to slide. This update is for everyone who ever wanted to be the Drift King.

+ NEW: DRIFT BOY, a new special career (Occupation -> Special Careers). Start in illegal street runs, move up to local tournaments, then the pro league. Four ranks: Street Racer, Local Circuit Driver, Pro Drift Driver, Drift King
+ NEW: Tandem battles. You lead, then you chase, and the judges score Angle, Speed and Line. Pick how hard you push at every corner, bring it home clean, or go for the wall. Tied battles end with One More Time
+ NEW: Tokyo (Daikoku and the Shuto), Hakone Turnpike, Fuji Speedway, a local circuit and an abandoned port lot, each one scored a different way
+ NEW: Any car in your garage can drift, and tuning counts. A Silvia S15, an AE86 Trueno and an RX-7 FD are the three best cars to start with. Roll cages and harnesses save the car and save you
+ NEW: 15 activities with 5 outcomes each: parking lot practice, track days, simulator, replays, reflex and fitness work, car meets, street takeovers, clips, tuning, your mechanic (sometimes a discount, sometimes a scam), sponsors, your team, trips to Japan
+ NEW: Sponsor contracts you negotiate, a co-driver and a mechanic you can hire, and 43 random events, from a scout in the crowd to an engine that finally gives up
+ NEW: Street heat. Takeovers and street battles raise it, and when it gets high the lights show up in your mirror. Drift away, pull over, or get arrested
+ NEW: JDM MODS, a new store in Shopping. 60 JDM drift cars (Silvias, Skylines, Evos, RX-7s, AE86s, Imprezas) with a new random selection, new and used, every year
+ NEW: 105 performance parts in 7 categories: turbos and engine swaps, intakes and cooling, coilovers and angle kits, brakes, wheels and tires, seats and safety, body and aero. One part per slot, each one adds drift rating
+ NEW: Motorcycles, dirt bikes and scooters (36 new bikes) at the motorcycle dealer, and every dealer now shows a fresh random selection each year. You can own several of the same model
+ NEW: Islands. Archipelago Estates in Shopping sells 24 private and wild islands with docks and beaches. Visit them from the Garage
+ NEW: The Champions League is now played, not skipped. Age locks, and every 1 Week is a match: 3 decisions in the group stage, 4 in the quarter-finals, 6 in the semi-finals and the final. Penalties you take yourself, free kicks, and shootouts when it is level
+ NEW: Mental Health and Insanity switch in Mods, to turn the bars, therapy and every Mental Health expansion off
+ NEW: 12 new luxury watches and 12 single-tooth grillz at the jewelers, shown on your tooth in the mirror
+ NEW: The Back button takes you to the exact screen you were on (Activities -> Salon & Spa -> Tanning goes back to Salon & Spa), and Mind & Body activities return to the same card
+ NEW: Cryptocurrency prices follow the real market from 2009 to 2025 in every mode. After that the big coins rise and fall but never disappear
+ NEW: A new random selection of vehicles at every dealer every year, not just the same list forever

- FIXED: The game freezing in Brazil, Mexico, Canada and every other country whose currency ends in a dollar sign
- FIXED: The Time Machine making your Mental Health and Insanity worse every time you used it
- FIXED: Pack hairstyles sitting too low and covering your eyes. Hair now finds your head and lines up with it
- FIXED: Dreads, emo, braids and rainbow hair looking like a rectangle around the face
- FIXED: The darkness in Find Heirloom moving with the flashlight instead of covering the attic
- FIXED: NBA Career showing two "declare for the draft" popups when you finish college
- FIXED: NBA Career losing your whole history and turning you back into a rookie when no team signs you
- FIXED: "Error / Error" on My Stuff and other buttons that were not ready. They say Coming soon
- FIXED: F12, Ctrl+Shift+S, Ctrl+U and the right-click menu being blocked on desktop
- FIXED: The showroom opening empty when you already owned the bikes it sold

- CHANGED: The Android APK is about 40 percent smaller. Images are compressed as WebP and sounds as OGG
- CHANGED: Islands, drift cars and parts are bought in Shopping. VIP and the main menu point there

! Life is simple. Win the touge, keep the heat low, and never lose the line.
